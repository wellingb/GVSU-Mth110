    1.

    The tortoise and the hare are racing. The tortoise starts at the starting line and runs at a constant speed. The hare also starts at the starting line but runs at a faster pace for several minutes. He then realizes that the tortoise is slow, so he stops to take a nap. When he wakes up, he sees that the tortoise has passed him, so he starts running again to the finish line.
    🔗

    (a)

    Which animal does each function represent in the graph? Label the graph with "Tortoise" and "Hare". Explain your thinking.
    🔗
    Answer.
    The graph that has a constant slope represents the tortoise because it is moving at a constant speed. The graph that increases, then is constant, and then increases again represents the hare because it starts off fast, then stops to take a nap, and then starts running again.

    • C. When the hare reached 800 meters, the fox was still at the starting line.

      🔗

    B and C are true. A is false because for the first 6 minutes, the fox was not moving. B is true because the fox traveled 1600 meters in 4 minutes, while the tortoise traveled 533.33 meters in that same time and the hare traveled 300 meters in that same time. C is true because when the hare reached 800 meters, it was at 6 minutes and the fox had not started yet.

    6.

    Let’s take a closer look at the story of the tortoise and the hare. Refer back to the first graph of the tortoise and the hare to answer the following questions
    🔗

    (a)

    What is the tortoises speed?
    🔗
    Answer.
    The tortoise’s speed is 133.33 meters per minute. Since his speed is constant, this is found by taking the distance (meter) and dividing by the amount of time it took to travel that distance. 1600 meters divided by 12 minutes equals 133.33 meters per minute.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (b)

    What is the hare’s average speed? (find all three speeds and divide by three)
    🔗
    Answer.
    The hare’s average speed is 122.22 meters per minute. This is found by taking the average of the three parts of his race. At the beginning he traveled 1300 meters in 6 seconds. Dividing this distance by the time, you find his speed is 1300/6 or 216.66 meters per minute. The second part of his race, he is resting so he has no speed or 0 meters per minute. The final part of his race, he travels 300 more meters in 2 seconds. Distance divided by time gives you 300/2 or 150 meters per minute. Averaging the three times (216.66+0+150)/3 gives you 122.22 meters per minute.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (c)

    Who won the race? How do you know?
    🔗
    Answer.
    The tortoise won the race. I know this because the tortoise’s average rate for the entire race was faster.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    (d)

    Now look back at the race between the tortoise, hare, and the fox. What is the fox’s speed? How do you know?
    🔗
    Answer.
    The fox started the race 6 minutes in and ended he race at 10 minutes after the start; meaning he ran for a total of 4 minutes. The total distance is 1600 meters. Distance divided by time gives 1600/4 = 400 meters per minute.
